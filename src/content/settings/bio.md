Fabio Zacco is a composer and multi-instrumentalist working across live performance and
music for picture. His writing moves between intimate acoustic textures and widescreen
orchestral color, shaped by years on stage as much as in the studio.

Recent work spans original scores for short film and documentary, session and live guitar
work with touring acts, and a growing catalog of solo material. He is currently based
between Milan and the road.
