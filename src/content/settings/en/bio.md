Fabio Zacco, musician and composer, was born in Ragusa, Sicily, in 1986. A graduate in
classical piano from the "Giacomo Puccini" Conservatory in Gallarate and in modern piano
and keyboards from CPM (Centro Professione Musica) in Milan, where he currently lives,
Fabio loves to range from classical music to jazz, from pop to rock, and from electronic
music to music for images.

As a session musician he has collaborated with **Biagio Antonacci**, accompanying him on
stage during the summer tour of 2023. He has performed with the youth orchestra "L.
Cherubini" conducted by **Maestro Riccardo Muti**, and with international popstar **Mika**,
with whom he played twice on the stage of the Sanremo Music Festival (2017 and 2020) and
at the "Mika Loves Beirut" (2020) and "Mika x Indeed x Pride" (2021) concerts.

He has also collaborated with rock band **Le Vibrazioni** (2025 tour), Giusy Ferreri,
Michele Bravi, Alexia (touring from 2017 to 2023), Loredana Bertè (2006 tour), Deborah
Iurato, Matteo Romano (2022 tour), Baltimora (2022 tour), Sister Cristina, Lodovica
Comello (European Tour 2015), and Lorenzo Fragola. He worked as a keyboard player on the
television program "D'IVA" with Iva Zanicchi on Canale 5 (2021), on two editions of "The
Voice of Italy" on Rai2 (2016 and 2018), on "Un palco per due" on Rai2 (2019), and on
several musicals including "Priscilla – Queen of the Desert," "Ghost," and "We Will Rock
You" by Queen and Ben Elton.

Beyond live performance, Fabio devotes himself to composition, arrangement, and
production. He has collaborated for several years with **Mediaset** on original music and
with **X-Factor** as an orchestrator, produces songs for emerging artists, and composed
additional music for the thriller "Daemon Mind," directed by French director \*\*Jason
Fite\*\*.

\*\*\*Fabio loves blending modern sounds, styles, and language with those of the classical
tradition, aiming to create increasingly distinctive sound worlds.\*\*\*
